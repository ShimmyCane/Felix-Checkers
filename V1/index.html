How to Play

  • Checkers: Move forward diagonally 1 square onto dark squares.
  • Jump: Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it!
  • Kings: Reaching the back row makes you a King 👑. Kings move backward and forward!
  • Felix Mode: Chess pieces live on the light squares (Bishops on B1 & F1, Queen on D1). Checkers can attack them forward and sideways!
